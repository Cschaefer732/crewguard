# House rules

This server is for the crew of Example Restaurant, a quick-service restaurant. It is not an official company channel. Joining is optional and does not affect your job.

Read this, then press **I Agree**. A manager gives you a role after that. The role is what unlocks the rest of the server.

## How chat is filtered

Messages in these categories are removed. The sender gets a DM with the reason, and every removal is logged where a manager can review it.

- **Profanity** — swearing aimed at a person.
- **Harassment / threats** — threats, intimidation, bullying, or repeatedly targeting a coworker.
- **Sexual content** — sexual messages or unwanted sexual attention.
- **Hostility toward a coworker** — contempt aimed at a coworker or manager: demeaning, belittling, snide, or passive-aggressive. No swearing required.
- **Gossip** — a coworker's personal life or drama when they aren't part of the conversation.
- **Off-topic (politics / selling)** — political or religious debate, and selling, fundraising, or promoting a side business.

Discord's own filter also blocks slurs.

Not filtered: venting about the job. The rush, the hours, being tired, the schedule, being short-staffed — complaining about work is fine.

## What happens next

The bot deletes the message, tells you why, and logs it. There are no strikes, timeouts, or bans from the bot. A manager decides anything further.
