# Shift pocket checklist

A generic opening and closing list for a quick-service restaurant. Replace it with your own store's list. This file is sample content so the Q&A bot has something to cite.

## Opening

1. Clock in and read the posted schedule.
2. Wash hands, tie hair back, and put an apron on.
3. Walk the line: stocked, labeled, and within date.
4. Check cold-holding and hot-holding before the first order.
5. Turn the order screen on and confirm the printer has paper.

## During the shift

1. Call out when a station is running low, and restock before it is empty.
2. Wipe surfaces as you go.
3. If a guest is hurt or a coworker is unsafe, tell a manager. Do not argue it out in chat.

## Closing

1. Break down the stations. Wash, rinse, then sanitize.
2. Label and date anything you put away.
3. Take out the trash, lock up, and tell the closer you are leaving.
